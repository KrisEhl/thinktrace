# Fusion Could Provide Electricity to the Grid by 2040

![Fusion Could Provide Electricity to the Grid by 2040]()

Commercial [fusion power](https://www.oakridger.com/story/news/2021/06/21/fusion-power-ornl-ter-project-oak-ridge-national-laboratory/7734017002/) – electricity from facilities that will exploit the same thermonuclear fusion process that enables the sun to provide the planet with life-sustaining heat and light – may no longer be 30 years away.

According to [Don Spong](https://www.oakridger.com/story/lifestyle/things-to-do/2024/06/16/next-orion-program-to-offer-a-look-at-smart-telescopes/73963995007/), a fusion theorist at Oak Ridge National Laboratory, electricity from a government-funded fusion pilot plant may be fed to the grid as early as 2040-2045.
